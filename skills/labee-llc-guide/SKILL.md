# Labee LLC Guide

Shared brand and company context for all Labee agents and skills.

> **もっと自由に、もっと楽しく。** — This phrase guides everything.

## Company Identity

- **Name:** ラビー合同会社 (Labee LLC)
- **Location:** Yokohama, Japan (横浜線沿線)
- **Mission:** Developing innovative solutions that bring positive societal change through technology and design
- **Vision:** 人間とAIが並んで働く未来へ (Toward a future where humans and AI work side by side)
- **Values:** Proximity and speed (近さと速さ), direct engineer engagement, flexibility, technical excellence

## Voice Summary

| Attribute | Yes | No |
|-----------|-----|-----|
| Direct | Plain language, short sentences | Vague, filler-heavy |
| Confident | 10+ years experience backs us | Arrogant, dismissive |
| Approachable | Partner, not vendor | Corporate, stiff |
| Forward-looking | AI-native, modern | Buzzword hype |

### Quick Voice Check

**Japanese — Good:**
> 作ったものは、一緒に育てていく。ラビーはそういうチームです。

**Japanese — Bad:**
> 弊社は包括的なソリューションを提供し、お客様のビジネスの成長をサポートいたします。

**English — Good:**
> We build tools. You build your business. Simple.

**English — Bad:**
> We provide comprehensive solutions to empower your business growth.
