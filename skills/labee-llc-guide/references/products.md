## Proprietary Products

### Chimr

**What:** Calendar and schedule management tool for macOS.

**Positioning:** Your day at a glance — meetings, events, and video calls without opening a full calendar app.

**Key features:**

- Today's events in a clean, minimal interface
- One-click join for video meetings (Zoom, Google Meet, Teams)
- Menu bar accessible — always visible, never in the way
- MCP server integration for AI-powered scheduling

**Target user:** Professionals who live in meetings and need quick access to their schedule.

### Vigilare

**What:** Task and reminder management tool for macOS.

**Positioning:** Tasks that stay visible. Not buried in an app you forget to open.

**Key features:**

- Apple Reminders integration (syncs with existing tasks)
- Desktop-native — always present, lightweight
- List management with archiving
- Priority levels and due dates
- MCP server for AI-powered task management

**Target user:** Anyone who needs their tasks visible without a heavy project management tool.

## Messaging Principles

1. **Lead with value, not features** — "Your day at a glance" not "Calendar integration with multi-platform support"
2. **Be specific** — "Syncs in under 200ms" not "Lightning-fast performance"
3. **Show the human side** — We're engineers who use our own tools
4. **Technology empowers** — AI gives people better tools, not replacement
5. **Partnership language** — "一緒に育てていく" (grow together), not "我々が提供する" (we provide)
